Introducing the [rock paper scissors battle royale](https://rps-battle.artnikitin.dev/)!

The rules are as follows: if you (an emoji) beat another emoji, then on collision that emoji joins your team. Simple and easy.

It allows customization of the emojis (or text) you'd like to add. You can change their personality, e.g. make them braver, make them slower, make them group up, etc.

There are some limitations. If you add an even number of emojis, there is no good way to allow a cyclical win system. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats rock, in a cycle. With 4, you either have 2 sets of neutral allies, or 2 sets of teams that beat each other. With 5, we can have a cycle, similar to rock-paper-scissors-lizard-Spock. The same goes for 7, 9, and any odd number. The game will update the cycles as more emojis get added.

For technical details, the emojis are simply [boids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boids). The difference is that by adjusting the 3 boid forces (separation, alignment and cohesion) you can get interesting and unique behaviours. I added 2 more forces: a repulsion from enemies and an attraction to prey. This combination of 5 forces gives each team a unique way of fighting. It becomes mesmerizing to see the teams fight.

Play around with it, mess with the settings, and enjoy!
